Turn Your Phone into a Full-Screen Scrolling LED Sign
HELLO LED

HELLO LED is a free LED banner app that turns your Android phone, tablet, or iPhone into a full-screen landscape LED sign. Create scrolling concert fan messages, airport pickup signs, and shop announcements without extra display equipment. Customize the message with five neon colors or a custom color, 11 fonts, adjustable text size and brightness, and scroll speed. Add flicker and glow effects, or show an LED clock, D-Day countdown, or timer.

How people use a smartphone LED sign

HELLO LED shows large text on a phone screen without extra display equipment. Use it for a concert fan sign, an airport pickup board, or a digital sign at a shop.

Concert LED fan sign

Show the cheer you want to send an artist on the phone LED sign. Type a short line or pick a preset, then set the neon color, font, and scroll speed so the sign reads in a dark venue.

Airport pickup sign

Show a name or a welcome line large on the phone banner. You do not need a paper sign: type the name, pick a color, and hold the phone up at arrivals.

Shop promotion and digital sign

Show a sale, opening hours, or pickup numbers at the door or the counter. Type a short line such as OPEN, SALE, or today's deal, and show it as scrolling LED text.

Birthdays and personal messages

Birthday, welcome, thank-you, and exam support lines work the same way. Presets cover eight kinds: cheers, birthdays, airport pickups, shops, love notes, exam support, welcomes, and thanks.

What the HELLO LED LED banner app can do

Use the phone as a scrolling LED sign. Set the text, color, and effect, and also show an LED clock, a D-Day countdown, a timer, or a photo in LED dots.

LED text color and scroll settings

From neon color to flicker

Use five preset neon colors (red, yellow, green, blue, pink) or a custom color. Adjust text size, brightness, and scroll speed, then pick flicker and glow effects and one of 11 fonts.

Questions

Can I turn a phone into an LED banner?

▾

Yes. HELLO LED turns a phone screen into a full-screen landscape LED sign without extra display equipment. Type a message and set the color, size, brightness, font, and scroll speed.

Can I use it as a concert fan sign?

▾

Yes. Type the cheer you want to send an artist, or pick a preset, then set the neon color, font, and scroll speed so the sign reads in a dark venue.

Can I use it as an airport pickup sign?

▾

Yes. Type the name or a welcome line and show it large on the phone. It works as a digital pickup sign at arrivals.

Can the text scroll on a phone?

▾

Yes. HELLO LED shows your message as a scrolling LED banner. Set the scroll speed and change the text color, size, and font too.

Can I show a clock, D-Day, or timer?

▾

Yes. Show the current time, a D-Day countdown, or a timer in large LED digits. Use it as a digital clock for a start time or the time left.

Can I convert a photo into LED dots?

▾

Yes. A gallery photo becomes an LED dot pattern on the banner. The conversion runs on the device, so the photo is not uploaded to a server.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

▾

Yes. It runs on Android phones and tablets and on iPhone. Install it from Google Play, new tab or the App Store, new tab.

Is HELLO LED free? Does it show ads?

▾

Yes, HELLO LED is free to install. A banner ad may appear on the editor screen, and an interstitial ad may appear before opening the full-screen LED sign. The full-screen sign that shows your LED message has no banner ad.

Can I save several messages or play them in order?

▾

Yes. Save favorites and play up to 8 messages in order. The screen stays on while the banner shows.

Are my photos or messages uploaded?

▾

No. Photo-to-dot conversion runs on the device, and messages and settings stay on the phone. Matching messages between HELLO LED devices on the same Wi-Fi also uses no external server.

Can I sync messages with another phone?

▾

Yes. HELLO LED apps on the same Wi-Fi can match messages. This also needs no external server.

Does it have an Android home screen widget?

▾

Yes. The Android home screen widget shows a saved message. The widget is available on Android only.
