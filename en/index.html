HELLO LED

HELLO LED is a free app that turns your phone into an LED board. Use it for concert cheers, shop sales, airport welcomes, and more.

What HELLO LED can do

Bright scrolling text and ready-made lines for cheers, promotions, and welcomes.

Color, speed, and font

From neon color to scroll speed

Pick red, yellow, green, blue, or pink, then match the scroll speed and typeface to a cheer, a sale, or a welcome.

App screen: landscape board text with color and typeface

HELLO LED

Full-screen landscape board

Easy to read from far away

Turn the phone sideways and the whole screen becomes an LED board, readable from the back of a venue or across a counter.

Clock, countdown, and timer

Show time instead of text

Put the current time, a countdown, or a timer on the board so people can read it from across a venue or shop.

App screen: clock on the landscape board

Photo to dot board

On-device image conversion

Turn a gallery photo into a dot pattern on the board. Photos stay on the device and are not uploaded.

App screen: photo converted to dots

How to turn a phone into an LED board

Anyone can use a phone as an LED board.

  1. 1

    Type a message

    Write a cheer, a sale, or a welcome, or pick a preset.

  2. 2

    Set color, speed, and font

    Choose a neon color, scroll speed, and typeface so it stays readable from a distance.

  3. 3

    Turn on full-screen landscape

    Turn the phone sideways and the whole screen becomes the board.
